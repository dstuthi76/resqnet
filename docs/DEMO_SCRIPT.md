# 3-minute demo script

**Before you go on stage**
- Wake the Render server: open the dashboard a few minutes early.
- Click **Reset**, then **Load demo messages**. Wait until the header shows about 64 cases.
- On the Relief page, click **Enrol demo households** and then **Submit satellite readings**. Don't click any NGO confirm buttons yet.
- Phones: phones 1–3 in airplane mode with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned back on, phone 4 online, ResQNet open on all of them.
- Backup: a screen recording of the full run, plus the **Mesh simulator** tab if the real phones misbehave. Say so if you use it.

| Time | Screen | Say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Early warning | "Hyderabad, October 2020. 190 mm of rain is forecast." Generate a **Telugu** alert and play it. "Every household hears it in their own language, before the water comes." |
| 0:30 | Phone 1 | "Towers are down. Phone 1 has no network." Send an SOS: 3 people, roof, medical, note "grandmother needs insulin". Show the *Mesh activity* log on phones 2 and 3 as it hops. |
| 1:00 | Phone 4 → dashboard | "Phone 4 has a signal." The SOS appears on the map near the **top**, with the reason: *medical: insulin · elderly · on roof*. |
| 1:20 | Dashboard | Claim it with the suggested NDRF boat team, ETA 40 min. Show phone 1: **"Help on the way: NDRF Boat Team 1, about 40 min"**, delivered back through the mesh. |
| 1:45 | Dashboard | "Meanwhile WhatsApp has 276 messages in five languages: the same families posted again and again, prayers, forwards." Point at the header: 276 messages → about 64 cases. Open a case to show one Telugu, one Tamil and one English report merged into one family. Try to claim it from a second browser: *already claimed*, so no two boats go to the same house. |
| 2:15 | Relief | "The water stays. Sentinel-1 radar sees through clouds: Malakpet was flooded for 96 hours." Two NGOs click **confirm** → **PAID OUT**. Open the transaction on Sepolia Etherscan. |
| 2:45 | Close | "No claim forms, no middlemen, a public audit trail. **Rescued in hours, relief in days, every rupee traceable.**" |

Be open about the limits: Android only, relaying only while the app is open, test ETH instead of UPI, and no licensed insurer yet. Present ResQNet as the platform insurers plug into.
